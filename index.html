FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you need? Book a demo and ask us directly.

Can Sumplace replace our current PSA and CRM?

Yes. Sumplace covers CRM, quoting, agreements, service desk, projects, purchasing and billing. We can migrate your companies, contacts, sites, tickets and products from your current system.

How long does set-up take?

A guided set-up reads your website, suggests your branches, modules and settings, and imports your data from spreadsheets with a preview. Most teams are working in Sumplace within a day.

Does it work with Xero?

Yes. Invoices, credit notes and payments sync to Xero, and Sumplace stays the system your team works in.

Where is our data hosted?

In Australia, on AWS, with each business in its own encrypted database, automated backups and copies kept in a second region.

What's the difference between Standard and Admin users?

Admins can change settings, manage users and permissions, use the API and connect integrations. Everyone else is a Standard user.
